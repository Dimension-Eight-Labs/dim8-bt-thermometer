## Modified and unofficial releases

You are welcome to fork, modify, and distribute the app. When you do:

- Do not claim or imply that your release is official or approved by Dimension Eight Labs LLC.
- Mark your changes as the GPL requires. Section 5(a) requires modified works to carry prominent notices stating that you modified them and giving a relevant date.
- Please identify yourself as the independent maintainer.
- Please use your own application ID and signing key.
- Where keeping the DIM8 name or logo could confuse people about who made your release, use distinguishable branding.

A clear statement such as "Unofficial fork of DIM8 BT Thermometer, maintained by Example Name" is a good way to do this.
